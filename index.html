AI Capabilities Showcase

An interactive demonstration of the AI features available in this environment.

1. AI Chat

Engage in a conversation with the AI. It can remember the last few messages to maintain context. Try asking it to write a poem or a short story!

2. JSON Output

The AI can be instructed to respond in a strict JSON format, making it easy to parse and use its output programmatically.

Click the button to see the JSON output.

3. Image Analysis (Vision)

The AI can analyze images and provide structured data about their content, again, using a JSON format for the response.

Click the button to analyze the image.

4. Image Generation

Generate unique images from a text prompt. The more descriptive your prompt, the better the result.

5. Text to Speech (TTS) & Voice Cloning

Convert text into audible speech using a variety of pre-set voices.
Additionally, you can try the experimental voice cloning feature. Upload a short, clear audio sample (.wav or .mp3, a few seconds is ideal) of a single voice, and the AI will attempt to use it to read your text.

— OR —

6. Sound & Music Generation
